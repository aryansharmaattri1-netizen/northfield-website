Content Production
THE 15-MIN REEL SYSTEM

High quality does not equal heavy editing. High quality means nail the hook, nail the audio, nail the framing, keep cuts at 2 seconds. That is it. One reel in 15-20 minutes from setup to export.

The Core InsightThe reels that go viral are not the most edited. They are the most clear. A great talking-head reel with good audio, strong hook text on frame 1, a cut every 1.5-2 seconds, and a muted background music track will outperform a heavily edited montage every single time for authority content.
The 15-Minute Production Workflow
1
Setup (2 min)
Dark background (cloth or wall). Ring light on, 45 above. Lapel mic clipped at chest. Phone on tripod at eye level. CapCut open. This setup takes 2 minutes once it has been done once.
2
Film (5-6 min)
Read the script 2x before filming. Hit record. Deliver it naturally - not read, not memorized. If you stumble: keep rolling, pause 2 seconds, restart the sentence. Edit out the stumble. 2-3 takes max.
3
Edit in CapCut (7-8 min)
Import best take. Cut every pause over 0.3 seconds. Every 1.5-2 seconds, there should be a meaningful cut or zoom. Add text hook on frame 1. Auto-captions: clean up errors. Color grade: lift shadows +10, contrast +8, saturation -5. Music: lo-fi ambient at 8% volume. Export.
What Makes a Reel Go Viral (5 Elements)
  • Frame 1 text hook - The first frame must show text that stops the scroll on mute. "The most dangerous thing for a founder..." on screen immediately. 85% of people watch muted.
  • Cut every 1.5-2 seconds - This is the single biggest retention driver. Every cut is a micro-reset. The brain re-engages. Do not cut on dead air - cut on energy words.
  • Audio quality first - Lapel mic is better than any ambient solution. If the audio is bad, nothing else saves the reel. This is non-negotiable.
  • Zoom punches on key words - At the 2-3 most important moments in the script, zoom in slightly (CapCut: keyframe scale from 100% to 105%). Creates emphasis without B-roll.
  • Sound design - One subtle sound effect on key transitions or the opening frame. CapCut free library has these. 1 second of a "whoosh" or "click" when text appears = significantly higher retention.
